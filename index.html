The migration

As winter fades, moose leave their winter grounds behind and follow ancient paths toward fresh grazing grounds.

Generation after generation, the migration continues. This spring, it's your turn. Shape and customize your own moose and become part of this ancient migration. wander through beautiful forests, cross lakes and streams, discover hidden places, and see what or who you stumble upon along the way.
