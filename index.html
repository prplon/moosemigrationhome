The migration

As winter fades, moose leave their winter grounds behind and wander through the forests, following ancient paths toward fresh grazing grounds. Generation after generation, the migration continues. This spring, it's your turn.

Shape and customize your own moose and become part of this ancient migration. Wander through beautiful forests, cross lakes and streams, discover hidden places, and see what or who you stumble upon along the way. Follow the path or wander off to explore, meet wildlife, encounter strange things in the woods, and get into all kinds of goofy situations.
