01 / The spring migration

Every spring, the moose walk the old path. This year you are one of them.

Every spring the moose of the Nordic forest leave their wintering grounds and walk the road they have always walked. Out of the deep pine. Over melting bog and cracking ice. Past sleeping villages and gardens that were not here a lifetime ago. Up to where the new green breaks.

Moose have walked it for thousands of years. None of them walked it like you will.
